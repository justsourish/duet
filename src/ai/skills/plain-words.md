# Plain words
Words in a design that say the thing and stop. No filler.

- Say what it is or what it does, in the fewest words that are still clear. Then stop.
- A button says what happens when it is pressed: "Order now", "Save", "Send". Never "Click here" or "Submit".
- Cut praise and padding: no "seamless", "powerful", "unlock", "elevate", "revolutionary", "effortless".
- A heading states one thing. A line under it adds one fact. Do not repeat the heading in other words.
- Use the reader's words, not the company's. Write to one person: "you", not "users".
- Use real, believable text in mockups: real names, real prices, real dates. Never lorem ipsum.
- When asked to tidy the words, keep the meaning and the tone, and make it shorter.
