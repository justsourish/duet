# Mobile screens
Sizes, safe areas and thumb reach for phone screens.

- A phone screen is a Frame. Common sizes: 390 x 844 (modern iPhone), 360 x 800 (common Android), 375 x 667 (small iPhone).
- Keep 16 px of space from the left and right edge of the screen to content.
- Leave the top 47 px for the status bar and the bottom 34 px for the home indicator. Do not put tappable things there.
- Anything tappable is at least 44 x 44 px, with at least 8 px between tappable things.
- Put the main action in the lower half of the screen, where a thumb reaches without stretching.
- One main action per screen. Make it the most visible thing.
- Body text is never smaller than 16 px on a phone.
