# Spacing
A steady rhythm of gaps, so layouts feel calm and intentional.

- Use an 8 px scale for gaps, padding and sizes: 4, 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64. Use 4 only for tight details.
- Things that belong together sit closer than things that do not. Space inside a group is smaller than space between groups.
- Padding inside a card is equal on all sides, or larger on the sides than on top and bottom. Never random.
- When asked to tidy spacing, snap sizes and gaps to the scale, keep alignment, and change as little else as possible.
- Align left edges of things stacked in a column. Do not leave two edges that are almost, but not quite, the same.
