# Bold blocks
A loud, flat look: thick outlines, hard shadows, bright colour. Off until you want it.

- Every card and button has a solid black stroke, 2 px, or 3 px on large containers.
- Shadows are hard, never soft: x 4, y 4, blur 0, colour #000000. Smaller things use 3 and 3.
- Fills are flat and bright: one warm paper background such as #FFFDF0, and one or two strong colours such as #FFE600 and #FF5C00. No gradients.
- Corners are square or barely rounded: radius 0 to 8.
- Text is black, heavy and big. Titles at weight 800, labels in capitals with a little letter spacing.
- Keep it to few colours. The contrast does the work, so do not add decoration.
