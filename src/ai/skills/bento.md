# Bento grid
A page laid out as a grid of rounded cards of different sizes, each holding one thing. Off until you want it.

- Build the page from cards on one grid: 12 columns on wide screens, 2 on a phone. A card spans whole columns.
- The same gap everywhere, 16 or 24 px, between cards and from the edge of the page.
- The same corner radius on every card, 20 to 28 px. The same padding inside every card, 24 px.
- Mix sizes on purpose: one large card for the main thing, a few medium, several small. No two rows the same.
- One idea in each card: a number, a picture, a short claim, a small chart. A title of a few words and at most two lines under it.
- Cards sit on a quiet background a little darker or lighter than the cards. One card may carry the strong colour.
