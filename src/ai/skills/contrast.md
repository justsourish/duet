# Colour contrast
Text people can actually read.

- Normal text needs a contrast ratio of at least 4.5 to 1 against its background. Large text (24 px or bigger) needs 3 to 1. This is the WCAG AA standard.
- To check, read the text colour and the colour of the shape it sits on from the design, then work out the ratio. Say clearly which text fails and suggest the closest colour that passes.
- Light grey text on white is the most common failure. Check it first.
- Do not rely on colour alone to show state. Add an icon, a label, or a shape change.
- When fixing contrast, change the text colour first. Only change the background if the text colour is a brand colour that must stay.
